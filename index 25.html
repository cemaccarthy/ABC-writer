tablature

notation guide: abc2svg

The score is engraved by abc2svg, a full ABC 2.1 engine that understands piano pedal marks, dynamics and hairpins. Playback uses abcjs and the instrument you pick in the footer. The score is laid out on A4 portrait sheets exactly as it will print: long scores continue on further pages (swipe sideways or use the arrows), and export pdf saves those same pages. Voices without a clef always use treble unless you write one (clef=bass), so clefs never change on their own.

K:C
[V:RH] !ped!CEGc e2 c2 !ped-up!| !ped!dfad' a2 f2 !ped-up!|]
[V:LH] !ped!C,2G,2 C2G,2 !ped-up!| D,2A,2 D2A,2|]

Playback follows the pedal: while it is down, notes you have already released keep sounding until the pedal comes up, and pressing it again clears the old sound. The downloaded midi file carries the sustain pedal too. Write the marks in the voice they belong to.

piano staves

%%score {RH | LH} joins two voices with a brace (right hand, left hand). Give each voice a clef with V:LH clef=bass, then write each line starting with [V:RH] or [V:LH].

editor colours

The editor colours notation the way a code editor colours code. Keywords (purple): field letters such as K:, %% directives, and repeat or ending marks. Types (teal): the key signature and voice names. Strings (green): titles, chord symbols, text annotations and lyrics. Numbers (orange): note lengths, meter, tempo and tuplets. Pitches (blue): the notes themselves. Operators (red): sharps, flats, naturals, octave marks, broken rhythm > and <. Decorators (gold): dynamics and ornaments, with the pedal underlined. Rests (brown). Punctuation (grey): bar lines, slurs, ties and brackets. Comments (grey italic). Grace notes are shown in italics.

ties

Write a hyphen straight after the first note: c4-c4, c4-|c4 or [ce]-[ce]. Both notes must be the same pitch, and a rest cannot be tied. A hyphen typed after a space (c4 -c4) or as a typographic dash is corrected for you.

instruments

Sounds come from a sampled General MIDI soundfont, so pianos and guitars are recordings of the real instruments. The first time you pick an instrument its samples download (a piano can take a few seconds), then they are cached. The menu is grouped into keys, guitar and plucked, bowed strings, voices, brass, woodwinds, bells, mallets and percussion, and synths. Struck and plucked sounds suit the sustain pedal best. To give each voice of a tune a different instrument, use the advanced tab.

advanced: an instrument for each voice

Give every voice in your tune its own sound, so a melody, a bass line and an accompaniment can play together on different instruments. Voices come from the V: lines in your notation. Anything left on default uses the instrument chosen in the footer. A choice made here overrides any %%MIDI program line, and it is also written into the downloaded midi file and wav.

Each new instrument downloads its own samples the first time it plays, so a tune with several instruments can take a little longer to start.
