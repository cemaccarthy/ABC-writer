notation guide: abc2svg

The score is engraved by abc2svg, a full ABC 2.1 engine that understands piano pedal marks, dynamics and hairpins. Playback uses abcjs and the instrument you pick in the footer. The score is laid out on A4 portrait sheets exactly as it will print: long scores continue on further pages (swipe sideways or use the arrows), and export pdf saves those same pages. Voices without a clef always use treble unless you write one (clef=bass), so clefs never change on their own.

K:C
[V:RH] !ped!CEGc e2 c2 !ped-up!| !ped!dfad' a2 f2 !ped-up!|]
[V:LH] !ped!C,2G,2 C2G,2 !ped-up!| D,2A,2 D2A,2|]

Playback follows the pedal: while it is down, notes you have already released keep sounding until the pedal comes up, and pressing it again clears the old sound. The downloaded midi file carries the sustain pedal too. Write the marks in the voice they belong to.

piano staves

%%score {RH | LH} joins two voices with a brace (right hand, left hand). Give each voice a clef with V:LH clef=bass, then write each line starting with [V:RH] or [V:LH].

editor colours

Header letters and values (key, meter, tempo, title) each have their own colour. In the music: pitches plain, accidentals red, octave marks light blue, durations green, rests grey, slurs and ties blue, bar lines brown, pedal blue underlined, dynamics green italic, chords red, text annotations green italic, comments grey italic.

ties

Write a hyphen straight after the first note: c4-c4, c4-|c4 or [ce]-[ce]. Both notes must be the same pitch, and a rest cannot be tied. A hyphen typed after a space (c4 -c4) or as a typographic dash is corrected for you.

instruments

Struck and plucked sounds suit the sustain pedal best: piano, electric piano, harpsichord, nylon guitar, pizzicato strings, harp, koto, music box, kalimba, celesta, glockenspiel, tinkle bell, tubular bells, vibraphone, marimba, xylophone, dulcimer and steel drums. Basic synth and warm synth pad are also available.
